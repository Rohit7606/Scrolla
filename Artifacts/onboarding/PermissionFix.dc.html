One permission needed
Scrolla measures distance through Android's Accessibility service — the same one screen readers use.
WHAT IT READS
How far a view scrolls, in pixels
Which app is in the foreground
The time of day it happened
WHAT IT NEVER SEES
What you are reading or watching
Text, images or passwords
Anything you type
WHERE IT GOES
Distance stays on this phone
Your group sees one number a day
The per-app split never leaves
