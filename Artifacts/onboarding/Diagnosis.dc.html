ONBOARDING · SIX PHASES · READ FROM SOURCE
The empty space is not a spacing bug. It is instructed.
RevealPhase puts Spacer(weight(0.25f)) above the content and Spacer(weight(0.75f)) below it — an explicit instruction to dump three quarters of the leftover height under the punchline. Five of the other phases use a plain weight(1f), so every screen has the same hole; Reveal is simply where it shows, because Reveal has the least content to hide it.
01 One skeleton, six times
Question, Reveal, Invitation, Permission, Battery and Join all read: content top-aligned, one weighted void, button pinned to the bottom.
Six screens that should feel like a narrative arc instead feel like one template with the words swapped.
02 Nothing scrolls
There is no verticalScroll anywhere in the file. PermissionPhase carries a headline, a subhead and nine bullets in three groups.
The file declares its own 2.0 font-scale preview. At that scale the permission screen cannot fit, and the button cannot be reached — onboarding dead-ends.
03 The ground stopped being a colour
Lines 125–169 give every phase its own glow x, y, radius and alpha. Reveal runs at 0.40, with a second radial underneath drawn at full-strength primary.
That is the muddy brown wash in your screenshot — and it is why the coral number reads soft: it is coral sitting on coral.
04 The guess is collected, then discarded
selectedGuess is rendered once, as grey text in a grey chip, and never referenced again.
You ask people to commit to an answer and then never tell them how wrong they were. Being out by 2800× is the most interesting fact in the whole flow and it is computed nowhere.
05 The accent bar beside the punchline
A 3dp coral rule, 52dp tall, at 30% alpha, sitting to the left of two lines of text.
It is the one decoration worth removing on sight, and it is doing nothing the two type weights are not already doing.
06 The peak is in the wrong place
Reveal is phase two of six. Four screens of permissions, battery settings and group codes follow it.
That order is probably right — earn the permission ask with the payoff — but it means Reveal is carrying the whole flow's motivation, and right now it is the emptiest screen in it.
CHOSEN · OPTION A, SCALE PROOF — WITH THE GLOW KEPT
The void becomes the argument, and the glow earns its place by coming from the thing being measured.
ANCHORED, NOT SCATTERED
One radial at the foot of the coral column, roughly 44% across and 66% down, so the light reads as emitted by the bar rather than smeared over the screen. The bar carries a matching bloom of its own.
CAPPED AT 0.24, NOT 0.40
And the second full-strength radial underneath is gone. That one was the actual source of the brown — it was drawn at full primary opacity at its centre.
A SCRIM OVER THE TOP THIRD
The number, the chip and the punchline all sit on near-black. Glow below the fold of the eye, type above it — that is what stops coral landing on coral.
