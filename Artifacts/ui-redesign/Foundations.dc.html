PEAK SCROLL TIME
Instrument Serif carries every number and title; Space Grotesk carries every label, row and paragraph. Two faces, six sizes, no inline overrides. Fallbacks are Georgia and the system sans — PNG and PDF export cannot embed webfonts, so both were chosen for close metrics.

Shape
Cards 20. Rows and inner blocks 14. Chips and bars fully round. Nothing else.
Motion
Nothing animates on arrival. The figure counts up once on a genuinely new value; bars grow when the day changes; tabs slide in the direction you travelled.
